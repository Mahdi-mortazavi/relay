Fixed: ping through Relay always succeeded and meant nothing.

Relay carries TCP and UDP, never ICMP — but the network stack was answering
pings for every address on the internet from inside the phone. A user spent an
evening on a DNS problem that was not a DNS problem because of it.

Pings to addresses Relay does not carry are now silent. Pinging the tunnel
itself still works, and is the right way to ask whether it is alive.
